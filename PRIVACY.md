# ArtikelFinder privacy policy

*Last updated: 28 September 2026*

**ArtikelFinder collects no data.** It does not collect, store, transmit, sell or share
any personal information, browsing history, page content or usage statistics.

## What the extension does on your computer

- **The dictionary ships inside the extension.** Every lookup runs locally; ArtikelFinder
  makes no network requests to any server, including our own (there is none).
- **It reads a page only when you ask.** When you choose *Artikel für „…“* from the
  right-click menu or press the keyboard shortcut, Chrome gives the extension temporary
  access to the current tab (the `activeTab` permission). ArtikelFinder reads the text
  you selected, looks it up, and shows the result next to it. It does not read or keep
  anything else, and it has no access to any page at other times.
- **Nothing is stored.** The selected word is not saved anywhere: not in the browser's
  storage, not in a file, not remotely. There are no cookies, analytics or tracking.

## Permissions

| Permission | Used for |
|---|---|
| `contextMenus` | The *Artikel für „…“* entry in the right-click menu |
| `activeTab` | Temporary access to the current tab after you click that entry or press the shortcut |
| `scripting` | Showing the result bubble in that tab and reading your selection for the shortcut |
