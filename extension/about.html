ArtikelFinder

Shows der, die or das for a selected German noun, including inflected forms, and says where every answer comes from.

How to use

Select a word on any page, then right-click → Artikel für „…“, or press Alt+Shift+A. If the shortcut does nothing, set it under chrome://extensions/shortcuts (Edge: edge://extensions/shortcuts).

Some words have several answers: Leitern is die Leitern (ladders) and also den Leitern (dative plural of der Leiter, leader). ArtikelFinder shows all of them instead of guessing, because the selected word alone cannot tell them apart.

How certain is an answer?

LabelMeaning
2 sources agreeUniMorph and Wikidata give the same gender.
1 source (Wikidata)The gender comes from Wikidata only.
1 source (UniMorph)The gender comes from UniMorph only; Wikidata has no matching entry.
Sources disagreeThe sources give different genders. ArtikelFinder shows Wikidata's, which was right in nearly every case we checked by hand, and names the other source's answer.
Gender unknownThe word is in the dictionary, but no source gives its gender.

Privacy

Everything runs on your computer. The dictionary ships inside the extension; ArtikelFinder makes no network requests, collects nothing, and only reads a page when you ask it to look up a word. Its permissions: context menus, and access to the current tab at the moment you use the menu entry or the shortcut.
